# Hypertension data

The hypertension data (Wang et al., 2005) included 7 studies comparing
the treatment effect of anti-hypertensive treatment versus control on
reducing diastolic blood pressure (DBP) in patients with hypertension.
Negative estimates indicate the reduction of DBP in the
anti-hypertensive treatment group.

## Usage

``` r
data(hyp)
```

## Format

A data frame with 10 rows and 2 variables

## Details

- `y`: Standardized mean difference

- `se`: Standard error

- `label`: Labels for each study

## References

Wang, J. G., Staessen, J. A., Franklin, S. S., Fagard, R., and
Gueyffier, F. (2005). Systolic and diastolic blood pressure lowering as
determinants of cardiovascular outcome. *Hypertension.* **45**(5):
907-913. <https://doi.org/10.1161/01.HYP.0000165020.14745.79>
